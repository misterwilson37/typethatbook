Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

TypeThatBook is a typing-practice website used in Computer Science classes in Sumner County Schools. This page explains what information it collects about students, why, who can see it, and how a parent or guardian can review it or have it deleted.

The short version
  • We collect only what a typing class needs: a student's name and school email (from their school Google account) and a record of their typing practice.
  • There are no advertisements. We never sell or rent student information, never use it for marketing, and never build profiles of students for any purpose other than the class.
  • Only the student, their teachers, school administrators and the site administrator can see a student's records.
  • Records are deleted after 24 months without any typing, or sooner on request.

How the school's permission works

TypeThatBook is used as part of classroom instruction, with the school district's approval. Under the federal Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), a school may give permission on parents' behalf for an educational service that is used only for school purposes. Because TypeThatBook's information is used only for the class, the school provides that permission. Parents and guardians keep the rights described below.

What we collect, and why

InformationWhy it is needed
Name and school email address, from the student's school Google account So the teacher can tell which student's work is which
An account ID (a random identifier created at sign-in) To connect a student's practice to their account
Class and school So each teacher sees only their own students
Typing practice: time spent, characters typed, speed, accuracy, and which book or lesson This is the practice the class grades
Lesson progress and reading position in each book So a student can pick up where they left off
Initials the student chooses, and game scores For the class leaderboard

We do not collect home addresses, phone numbers, birthdates, photos, audio or video, location, student ID numbers, or any health, disability or demographic information. A student is never asked for more information than taking part in the class requires.

A student who uses TypeThatBook without signing in is a guest. A guest's progress is kept only in their own browser and nothing about them is stored on our servers. If that student later signs in on the same browser, their guest practice is added to their account.

Who can see it

  • The student can see their own records.
  • Their teachers can see the records of students in their classes.
  • School administrators can see records for students at their schools.
  • The site administrator can see everything, in order to run the site.
  • Other students can see only a student's chosen initials and typing scores on the leaderboard — never their name or email. Students can hide themselves from the leaderboard in their settings.

These limits are enforced on Google's servers for every request, not just in the website, so they cannot be bypassed from a browser.

Parents' and guardians' rights

A parent or guardian may at any time:

  • review the information TypeThatBook holds about their child;
  • ask for it to be deleted; and
  • ask that no further information be collected from their child.

To make a request, contact the email address above or ask your child's school. So that a child's information is only ever given to their own parent or guardian, we confirm the request through the school before acting on it. Requests are completed within 30 days. A student whose information has been deleted will start TypeThatBook from the beginning if they use it again.

How long information is kept

Typing records are kept while a student is actively using TypeThatBook, and are deleted after 24 months with no typing of any kind. Computer Science is taught in nine-week rotations, so a student can go more than a year between one grade's class and the next; keeping records for 24 months means a returning student keeps their lesson progress, while the records of students who have left are removed. Information is also deleted sooner whenever a school or parent asks.

How it is protected

Every request to the database is checked against security rules, all traffic is encrypted, students sign in with their school Google accounts (TypeThatBook never sees a password), and staff can see only the students they need to. The full written security program is published as TypeThatBook's security program.

Changes to this policy

If TypeThatBook ever starts collecting a new kind of information or using it in a new way, this page will be updated first and the school will be told. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
