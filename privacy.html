Privacy & Data Policy

Effective September 2026

TypeThatBook is a typing-practice website used in Computer Science classes in Sumner County Schools. It was created, and is run, by Jake Wilson, a Sumner County Schools Computer Science teacher. This page explains what information TypeThatBook collects about students, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how a parent or guardian can review it or have it deleted.

In short
  • TypeThatBook collects only what a typing class needs: a student's name and school email (from their school Google account) and a record of their typing practice.
  • No advertising. No data mining. No selling. Student information is never sold, rented, used for marketing, or used to build profiles of students for any purpose other than the class.
  • Only the student, their teachers, school administrators and the site administrator can see a student's records.
  • Accounts with no typing for two years are deleted in a quarterly check, and any student's information is deleted sooner on request.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Many TypeThatBook users are under 13, so it is covered by the federal Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). This page is TypeThatBook's notice under that law. TypeThatBook is used as part of classroom instruction with the approval of the school. Under COPPA, a school may consent on parents' behalf to an educational service that is used only for school purposes and never for commercial purposes; TypeThatBook is used only for the class, and the school provides that consent. Before their class uses TypeThatBook, each teacher confirms that their school has approved it and consents on parents' behalf, and that confirmation is recorded with its date. Parents and guardians keep the rights described under Parents' and guardians' rights, below.

Tennessee's Student Online Personal Protection Act also applies, to students of every age. TypeThatBook follows it: no targeted advertising, no profiles built for non-school purposes, no sale of student information, reasonable security, and deletion when the school asks.

TypeThatBook does not collect home addresses, phone numbers, birthdates, photos, audio or video, location, student ID numbers, or any health, disability or demographic information. Students never create a password on TypeThatBook, and are never asked for more information than taking part in the class requires.

How information is used

Student information is used only to run TypeThatBook for the class: to save each student's progress, and to show teachers how much their students have practised. It is not used for advertising, is not analysed or "mined" for any other purpose, is not sold or rented, and is not shared with anyone for marketing. TypeThatBook uses no analytics or tracking services.

Who can see it

  • The student can see their own records.
  • Their teachers can see the records of students in their classes.
  • School administrators can see records for students at their schools.
  • The site administrator can see everything, in order to run the site.
  • Other students can see only a student's chosen initials and typing scores on the leaderboard — never their name or email. Students can hide themselves from the leaderboard in their settings.
  • After a student leaves. If a student's account is deleted because they haven't used TypeThatBook for 24 months, any leaderboard place they still hold stays until someone beats it — showing only their chosen initials, the score, the date it was set and their school. It is no longer connected to their account, and students who hid themselves from the leaderboard are not kept.

These limits are enforced on Google's servers for every request, not just in the website, so they cannot be bypassed from a browser.

Cookies and browser storage

TypeThatBook uses no advertising or tracking cookies. To keep a student signed in, Google's sign-in service stores a token in the browser. TypeThatBook also saves some things in the browser itself — a guest's progress, settings, and temporary copies of data so pages load faster. Google reCAPTCHA may set a cookie as part of its security check.

Students who don't sign in

A student who uses TypeThatBook without signing in is a guest. A guest's progress is kept only in their own browser, and nothing about them is stored on TypeThatBook's servers. If that student later signs in on the same browser, their guest practice is added to their account.

How long information is kept

Typing records are kept while a student is actively using TypeThatBook, and are deleted after 24 months with no typing of any kind — in the Library, the lessons or the games. Accounts are checked every quarter. Computer Science is taught in nine-week rotations, so a student can go more than a year between one grade's class and the next; keeping records for 24 months means a returning student keeps their lesson progress, while the records of students who have left are removed. An account with no typing ever recorded is reviewed individually rather than removed automatically, since it may belong to a new student who has not started yet.

When a parent or the school asks for a student's information to be deleted, everything is removed: their typing records, lesson progress, reading positions, game scores — including any leaderboard place — and their TypeThatBook sign-in account. When an account is deleted because it hasn't been used for 24 months, the same is removed, except that a leaderboard place the student still holds is kept by their initials alone, as described under "Who can see it". A student's sign-in account is deleted once their records have been removed and the account hasn't been used for 24 months.

Parents' and guardians' rights

A parent or guardian may at any time:

  • review the information TypeThatBook holds about their child;
  • ask for it to be deleted; and
  • ask that no further information be collected from their child.

To make a request, email privacy@misterwilson.org, call (615) 379-7226, or contact your child's school. So that a child's information is only ever given to their own parent or guardian, we confirm the request through the school before acting on it. Requests are completed within 30 days. A student whose information has been deleted will start TypeThatBook from the beginning if they use it again.

How it is protected

Every request to the database is checked against security rules, all traffic is encrypted, students sign in with their school Google accounts (TypeThatBook never sees a password), and staff can see only the students they need to. The full written security program is published as TypeThatBook's security program. If student information were ever accessed by someone who should not have it, the school would be told promptly.

Teacher and staff accounts

Teachers and administrators also sign in with their school Google accounts. TypeThatBook stores their name, school email, role, and the schools and classes they are assigned to, so that each person sees only the students they should.

Changes to this policy

If TypeThatBook ever starts collecting a new kind of information or using it in a new way, this page will be updated first and the school will be told. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
